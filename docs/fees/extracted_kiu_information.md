## HOW TO APPLY

1. **Application Forms**: Available at the Admission Office at the Main Campus in Kansanga, Kampala
2. **Application Fee**: USD 25
3. **Online Application**: www.kiu.ac.ug (fee paid at submission)
4. **Required Documents**:
   - Three photocopies each of 'O' and 'A' Level result slips or equivalents
   - Three certified copies of transcripts (for Postgraduate degrees)
   - Receipt for application form payment
   - Three recent passport size photographs
   - Recommendation letter from previous school or employer
   - Photocopy of Birth Certificate

**Contact**: THE DIRECTOR OF ADMISSIONS
Kampala International University
P.O. BOX, Kampala - Uganda
Tel: +256 414 100808
Website: www.kiu.ac.ug

## Summary

This compilation includes information about KIU's programs across multiple faculties:

1. **Business and Management** - Diplomas to PhDs
2. **Social Sciences and Humanities** - Comprehensive programs in social work, public administration, international relations
3. **Law** - Full range from Diploma to PhD
4. **Computer Science and IT** - Modern programs including AI, Cyber Security, Data Science
5. **Education** - Teacher training at all levels
6. **Engineering** - Various engineering disciplines
7. **Health Sciences** - Public Health programs
8. **Environmental Science** - Conservation and environmental management
9. **Agriculture** - Agribusiness and agricultural economics

**Note**: The information shows two fee structures:
- **International Campus**: Fees in USD (generally higher)
- **Local Campus**: Fees in UGX (generally lower for local students)

Contact KIU for the most current information and specific admission requirements.
