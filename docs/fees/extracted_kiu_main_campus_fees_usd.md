## HOW TO APPLY

Application forms are available at the Admission Office at the Main Campus in Kansanga, Kampala.

Or apply online at www.kiu.ac.ug. Application fee for application forms downloaded online should be paid at submission of the application form.

### Required Documents:
1. Three photocopies each of 'O' and 'A' Level result slips or equivalents (Diploma and Bachelor Degree) and three certified copies of transcripts of Bachelor and Master Degrees (Postgraduate degrees).
2. A photocopy of a receipt for payment for the application form.
3. Three recent passport size photographs enclosed in small envelope.
4. Recommendation letter from either the previous school or employer.
5. Photocopy of Birth Certificate.
