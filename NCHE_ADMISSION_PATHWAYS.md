# NCHE Uganda - Complete Admission Pathways

## Education Levels in Uganda (Highest Qualification)

### 1. National Certificate (TVET Level 2-3)
- **Duration**: 1-2 years
- **Entry**: O-Level with 3-4 passes
- **Awarding Bodies**: DIT (Directorate of Industrial Training), UBTEB
- **Can Lead To**: Diploma, HEC, Certificate programs

### 2. O-Level (UCE - Uganda Certificate of Education)
- **Duration**: 4 years (S1-S4)
- **Exit**: UCE Examination (UNEB)
- **Can Lead To**: A-Level, Certificate, HEC (if old enough)

### 3. A-Level (UACE - Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education)
- **Duration**: 2 years (S5-S6)
- **Exit**: UACE Examination (UNEB)
- **Grading**: Principal (A-E) + Subsidiary (O)
- **Can Lead To**: HEC, Diploma, Bachelor (with sufficient grades)

### 4. Higher Education Certificate (HEC)
- **Duration**: 9 months - 1 year
- **Tracks**: Arts, Biological, Physical
- **Entry**: 2 Subsidiary passes OR 1 Principal pass at A-Level
- **Can Lead To**: Bachelor Degree

### 5. Diploma (TVET Level 5-6 / Academic)
- **Duration**: 2 years
- **Entry**: 
  - A-Level: 1 Principal + 2 Subsidiaries
  - Or Certificate in relevant field
- **Can Lead To**: Bachelor Degree (with credit/distinction)

### 6. Bachelor Degree (TVET Level 7 / Academic)
- **Duration**: 3-5 years
- **Entry**:
  - Direct: A-Level with 2 Principal passes
  - Diploma: Relevant diploma (Credit/Distinction)
  - HEC: HEC completion in relevant track
- **Can Lead To**: Masters

### 7. Masters (Postgraduate)
- **Duration**: 1.5-2 years
- **Entry**: Bachelor Degree (Second Class Lower or above)
- **Can Lead To**: PhD

### 8. PhD (Doctoral)
- **Duration**: 3-5 years
- **Entry**: Masters Degree

## Detailed Entry Requirements (NCHE Compliant)

### 1. National Certificate Programs

**Minimum Entry:**
- UCE with at least 3 passes (D1-C6 or A-C in new curriculum)
- OR Completion of Junior School (PLE)
- For some technical programs: Pass in Mathematics and English

**Common Programs:**
- Certificate in Business Administration
- Certificate in Information Technology
- Certificate in Agriculture
- Certificate in Education (Primary)

---

### 2. Higher Education Certificate (HEC)

**NCHE Requirements:**
- UACE with at least 2 subsidiary passes (Principal subjects at O level)
- OR 1 principal pass
- OR Vocational qualifications at Level 2 or 3
- OR Grade III Teacher's Certificate

**HEC Tracks & Progression:**

| HEC Track | Prepares For | Duration | Progresses To |
|------------|--------------|----------|---------------|
| **HEC Arts** | Humanities, Social Sciences, Business, Law | 9-12 months | BA, BBA, BCom, LLB, BSW, BPA |
| **HEC Biological** | Health Sciences, Agriculture, Environment | 9-12 months | MBChB, BNSc, BPharm, BMLS, BDS, BPH |
| **HEC Physical** | Engineering, Technology, Sciences | 9-12 months | BSE, BEE, BME, BCS, BIT, BSc |

**HEC Admission Subjects Required:**
- Arts: No specific subjects required
- Biological: Biology at O-Level or any science subject
- Physical: Mathematics and Physics at O-Level preferred

---

### 3. Diploma Programs

**NCHE Requirements:**
- UACE with at least 1 principal pass and 2 subsidiary passes
- OR Certificate in relevant field from recognized institution
- OR HEC completion (for some diploma programs)

**Diploma Classifications:**
- **Distinction**: 70-100% (Direct degree entry with credit transfer)
- **Credit**: 60-69% (Direct degree entry)
- **Pass**: 50-59% (Degree entry with conditions)

**Common Diploma Programs:**
- Diploma in Business Administration
- Diploma in Computer Science
- Diploma in Education (Primary/Secondary)
- Diploma in Nursing
- Diploma in Clinical Medicine
- Diploma in Pharmacy
- Diploma in Engineering (various)

**Diploma to Degree Progression:**
- Diploma holders enter Year 2 or 3 of degree (credit transfer)
- Must be in related field
- Minimum Credit grade required for most programs

---

### 4. Bachelor Degree Programs

**NCHE Requirements - Direct Entry:**
- UACE with at least 2 principal passes in relevant subjects
- Total points: Minimum 6 (2 Es) to 18 (3 As)
- General Paper recommended for all programs

**NCHE Requirements - Diploma Entry:**
- Relevant diploma (Credit or Distinction)
- Diploma must be from NCHE-recognized institution
- Subject matter must be related

**NCHE Requirements - HEC Entry:**
- HEC completion in relevant track
- Minimum pass in HEC program

**NCHE Requirements - Mature Age Entry:**
- Age 25+ with demonstrated competence
- Pass Mature Age Entry Exam
- Relevant work experience

**Subject Requirements by Program Category:**

| Program Category | Essential Subjects | Minimum Points |
|------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Medicine (MBChB, BDS)** | Biology + Chemistry | 15-16 |
| **Nursing/Pharmacy** | Biology + Chemistry/Physics | 10-12 |
| **Engineering** | Mathematics + Physics | 12-13 |
| **Computer Science/IT** | Mathematics | 8-10 |
| **Law** | Arts subjects (History/Lit/Geog/Econ) | 10-11 |
| **Business** | Mathematics preferred | 6-8 |
| **Education** | Relevant teaching subjects | 6-8 |

---

### 5. Masters Programs

**NCHE Requirements:**
- Bachelor's Degree (Second Class Lower or above)
- Some programs require: Second Class Upper
- Work experience: 2+ years for MBA
- Pre-entry exams: Some programs (MBA, MEd)

**Masters Types:**
- **Taught Masters**: Coursework + dissertation
- **Research Masters**: Thesis-based
- **Professional Masters**: MBA, MPH, MEd

---

### 6. PhD Programs

**NCHE Requirements:**
- Master's Degree (Coursework or Research)
- Research proposal
- Interview with supervisors
- Publication record (for competitive programs)

## Special Pathways

### 1. TVET (Technical and Vocational) Pathway
```
PLE → Junior School → O-Level → National Cert → Diploma → Bachelor
     ↓
   Junior Cert → TVET Cert → Diploma → Bachelor
```

### 2. Academic Pathway
```
PLE → O-Level → A-Level (good grades) → Bachelor → Masters → PhD
             ↓
           A-Level (low grades) → HEC → Bachelor
             ↓
           A-Level (mid grades) → Diploma → Bachelor
```

### 3. Mixed Pathway
```
O-Level → Certificate → Diploma → Bachelor → Masters
```

### 4. Professional Progression
```
Grade III Teacher → Diploma in Education → BEd → MEd → PhD
```

## Implementation Guide for System

### 1. Qualification Assessment Logic

```python
def assess_qualification(highest_education, grades, certificates):
    pathways = []
    
    if highest_education == "olevel":
        if grades.passes >= 5:
            pathways.append("national_certificate")
            pathways.append("certificate")
    
    elif highest_education == "alevel":
        if grades.principal_passes >= 2:
            pathways.extend(["bachelor", "diploma", "hec"])
        elif grades.principal_passes >= 1 and grades.subsidiary_passes >= 2:
            pathways.extend(["diploma", "hec"])
        elif grades.subsidiary_passes >= 2:
            pathways.append("hec")
    
    elif highest_education == "hec":
        if certificates.hec_completed:
            pathways.append("bachelor")
    
    elif highest_education == "diploma":
        if certificates.diploma_class in ["credit", "distinction"]:
            pathways.append("bachelor")
        pathways.append("diploma_upgrade")
    
    elif highest_education == "bachelor":
        if grades.degree_class in ["first", "second_upper", "second_lower"]:
            pathways.append("masters")
        pathways.append("bachelor_2nd_degree")
    
    elif highest_education == "masters":
        pathways.append("phd")
    
    return pathways
```

### 2. Program Recommendation Logic

```python
def recommend_programs(pathways, subjects, interests):
    recommendations = []
    
    if "bachelor" in pathways:
        if "Biology" in subjects and "Chemistry" in subjects:
            recommendations.extend(["MBChB", "BDS", "BPharm", "BNSc"])
        if "Mathematics" in subjects and "Physics" in subjects:
            recommendations.extend(["BSE", "BEE", "BME"])
        if "Mathematics" in subjects:
            recommendations.extend(["BCS", "BIT", "BCom"])
    
    if "hec" in pathways:
        if any(s in subjects for s in ["Biology", "Chemistry"]):
            recommendations.append("HEC_BIOLOGICAL")
        elif any(s in subjects for s in ["Physics", "Mathematics"]):
            recommendations.append("HEC_PHYSICAL")
        else:
            recommendations.append("HEC_ARTS")
    
    if "diploma" in pathways:
        recommendations.extend(["DIP_BUSINESS", "DIP_CS", "DIP_EDUCATION"])
    
    return recommendations
```

## Summary

This system handles the complete education spectrum in Uganda:

1. **Starting Point**: O-Level (minimum requirement for higher education)
2. **Bridges**: HEC and Diploma (for those with insufficient A-Level grades)
3. **Main Goal**: Bachelor Degree (standard university education)
4. **Advanced**: Masters and PhD (postgraduate)

The system ensures **NCHE compliance** by:
- Checking minimum entry requirements for each level
- Verifying subject prerequisites
- Validating previous qualifications
- Tracking curriculum versions (old/new)
- Supporting multiple entry pathways
