## Payment Information

### How to Pay
1. **Bank Transfer** to KIU official accounts
2. **Mobile Money** (MTN, Airtel) - for local students
3. **Online Payment** via KIU portal: www.kiu.ac.ug
4. **Bank Deposit** at any bank branch

### Payment Deadlines
- **Application Fee**: Due at time of application submission
- **Tuition & Functional Fees**: Due at beginning of each semester
- **Late Payment**: May attract penalties or denial of registration
